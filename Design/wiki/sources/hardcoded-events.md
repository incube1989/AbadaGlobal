# Source — Hardcoded Events Data

Summary of the normalized seed data in
[`Design/Increment/hardcodedEvents.md`](../../Increment/hardcodedEvents.md)
(archived as `Design/Archive/hardcodedEvents.md`).

Seed events for 2026–2027 used by the MVP calendar. Normalized to ISO date
ranges, standard English place names, and the type/scope vocabulary of
[[concepts/calendar-spec|Calendar Spec]].

## Highlights

- 2026: Capoeira na Beira do Volga (Samara), Voronezh and Saint Petersburg
  competitions, Festival Cultural (Saint Petersburg, Prof. Tuka e equipe)
- 2027: Samara open competition, Moscow Region Batizado Integrado, Jogos
  Europeus (Luxembourg), Batizados in Saint Petersburg, Voronezh, Samara and
  Yekaterinburg, Moscow competition
- Camps: CapoWeekend 14+/adults and kids camp 7–13 y.o. (Moscow Region,
  June–July 2027)
- Zumbimba — the whole month of November every year, in Rio de Janeiro
- Jogos Mundiais (World Games 2027) — August 2027, exact dates and city TBD

Values marked with * in the source file are inferred from context and will be
refined later; the MVP calendar is built to test the calendar itself.
