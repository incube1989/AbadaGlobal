In capoeira we have belt system and titles.
Belt is named "corda", in inglish it is the same "corda" and in russian it is "корда"

Every corda has a name that specifies 2 colors. At some point corda grants title. Corda names and titles should not be translated into other languages and stay in portuguese

no title:
Crua - both light grey
Cura-amarela - light greay + yellow
Amaraela - both part are yellow
Amarela-laranja - yellow and orange
Laranja - both colors are orange
Laranja-Azul - orange and blue


Graduado level:
- azul - two blue colors
- azul-verde - blue and green
- verde - both green
- verde-roxa - green and purple

Instructor level:
- roxa - both purple
- roxa-marrom  - purple and brown

Professor level:
- marrom - both brown
- marrom-vermelha - brown and red


Mestrando
- vermelha-branco - red and white


Mestre
- vermelha-branco - red and white

Grao mestre
- branco - both white
